# Code of conduct

Kaveyl Wiki follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we want a friendly, welcoming community for everyone – regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others’ private information are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously violate it.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **me@kaveyl.app**. Every report is reviewed and handled confidentially.
